Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we store

Your trips, plans, visited places, reviews, photos and settings. These are kept on your device and, so they survive reinstalling and reach your other devices, mirrored to our backend (Supabase) against your account.

Signing in

You sign in with Apple or Google. We receive an account identifier and, if you allow it, an email address. Apple's Hide My Email gives us a relay address rather than your real one — that is fine, we never need it. We never see your password. You choose a nickname, and you can add a name and a profile photo.

Location

Location is used to show what is near you, to centre the map, and — only if you turn on auto check-in — to notice when you arrive at a stop on your Live Trip. Your location is used on the device and is not sold or shared. Auto check-in asks for "Always" access because arriving somewhere has to register with the app closed; you can turn it off in Live Trip or in iOS Settings.

Photos

Photos you attach to a plan stay on your device. Photos you post to the Travel Feed or add to a comment, and your profile photo, are uploaded so other people can see them. Deleting a post deletes its photos.

Contacts

Only if you choose to find friends from your contacts. The phone numbers and emails in your address book are scrambled (hashed) on your phone, and only the hashes are sent, to check which belong to people already on Triproute. They are compared and thrown away — never stored — and names and the contacts themselves never leave your phone. If you let friends find you by your own number or email, a hash of that one is stored, and no one can read it.

Microphone and speech

Only when you talk to the travel assistant. Apple's speech recognition turns what you say into text; we do not record or keep the audio. The assistant itself runs on your device with Apple Intelligence — your questions are not sent to us or to an AI company.

Calendar and notifications

The calendar is used only to add your trip's stops, when you ask. Notifications remind you about upcoming trips and tell you about trip invites; to deliver an invite we store your device's push token against your account.

Services we ask for information

Place searches, directions and place details come from Apple Maps; photos and descriptions from Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and Wikivoyage; flight details, when you look up a flight, from AeroDataBox through our server. These requests carry the place, area or flight you asked about — not your name or account.

What other people can see

Your nickname, name and profile photo, and what you post to the Travel Feed — posts, photos, comments and likes. Your trips, plans, visited places and notes are private unless you post or share them, and a shared trip is seen only by the people you invite. You can block anyone to hide their posts and comments from you.

Analytics and advertising

None. Triproute has no third-party analytics, no advertising and no tracking SDKs, and we never sell your data.

Deleting your data

Profile → Delete account removes your account and the data stored against it, including your posts, comments, likes and their photos. This cannot be undone.

Children

Triproute is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here and on our website with a new date.
